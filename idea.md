# Project Idea - My Notebook: Personal Knowledge Management & Learning Platform

## 1. Project Overview
A comprehensive full-stack solution designed for students and professionals to centralize their entire learning journey. The platform enables users to organize, annotate, and transform static content (notes, documents, images, and videos) into actionable knowledge through AI-powered enhancements and sophisticated organizational tools.

## 2. Core Objectives
- **Centralization**: Serve as a unified vault for years of multi-format academic or professional content.
- **Enhanced Learning**: Leverage AI to generate active recall materials (MCQs) and concise summaries automatically.
- **Creative Freedom**: Provide professional-grade annotation and drawing tools directly within the knowledge base.
- **Accessibility**: Enable full-text search across all content types, including archived media metadata.

## 3. Key Features

### 3.1 Advanced Content Organization
- **Hierarchical Structure**: Organize materials by Subjects → Topics → Notes/Files with a nested folder structure.
- **Dynamic Management**: Create, move, rename, or delete files and folders anywhere in the tree.

### 3.2 Hybrid Input Support
- **Rich Text Engine**: Full-featured editing for manual notes including text, images, and embedded media.
- **Handwritten Digits**: Support for uploading and archiving handwritten notes as images or PDFs.
- **Media Vault**: Secure storage for video lectures and high-resolution images via Google Drive API.

### 3.3 Annotations & Drawing
- **Canvas Overlay**: Highlight and draw on notes using customizable colored pencils and varied thicknesses.
- **Persistence**: Annotation data is saved as high-precision metadata for non-destructive editing.

### 3.4 AI Learning Services
- **Automated MCQ Generation**: Generate 5 initial MCQs per source with toggle-able answer transparency. Users can generate additional questions as needed.
- **Intelligent Summarization**: Distill complex documents into concise summaries using LLMs.

### 3.5 Global Search & Social
- **Recent Activity**: Dashboard tracking of recent notes and edits.
- **Full-Text Indexing**: Instantly search titles and content bodies across the entire platform.
- **Tokenized Sharing**: Secure, read-only note sharing via expiration-capable tokenized URLs.
- **Universal Export**: Download any material in Markdown or PDF formats for offline use.
